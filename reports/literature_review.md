# Background Research and Literature Review

This file provides a concise background reading list for the economic bubble dashboard.

## Classic bubble frameworks
- Kindleberger, Charles P., and Robert Aliber. *Manias, Panics, and Crashes*.
- Minsky, Hyman P. *Stabilizing an Unstable Economy*.
- Shiller, Robert J. *Irrational Exuberance*.

## Financial crises and fragility
- Reinhart, Carmen, and Kenneth Rogoff. *This Time Is Different*.
- Mian, Atif, and Amir Sufi. Research on leverage, housing, and credit cycles.
- Jordà, Òscar, Moritz Schularick, and Alan Taylor. Research on credit booms and macro-financial instability.

## Bubbles, valuation, and speculation
- Greenwood, Robin, Andrei Shleifer, and Yang You (2019). *Bubbles for Fama*.
- De Bondt and Thaler on overreaction and reversals.
- Jegadeesh and Titman on momentum.

## Machine learning and financial forecasting caveats
- Gu, Kelly, and Xiu (2020). Empirical asset pricing via machine learning.
- López de Prado, Marcos. *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*.
